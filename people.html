People

A networked lab.

Our team connects human–computer interaction, design, cognitive science, data sensemaking, biomedical signal processing, and human–machine systems across three Dutch universities.

Christina SchneegassLab lead · TU Delft

Assistant Professor at TU Delft’s Faculty of Industrial Design Engineering. Her research examines human cognition in HCI, with a focus on cognitive personal informatics, consumer neurotechnology, and interfaces that help people interpret cognitive data responsibly.

Dimitra DritsaLab co-lead · TU Delft

Researcher at TU Delft’s Faculty of Industrial Design Engineering. She works at the intersection of data sensemaking, affective and physiological computing, and information visualisation, including how people interpret gaps between wearable data and their expectations.

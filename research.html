Research

Between measure and meaning.

We study what happens when cognitive and physiological measurements move into everyday life: how claims are made, how people interpret them, and how interactive technologies can support careful sensemaking.

01 / Directions

01

Sensing the mind

We investigate consumer neurotechnology and physiological sensing without treating measurements as transparent representations of mental states.

02

Making sense of cognition

We examine how people interpret cognitive data, live with uncertainty, and develop stories about attention, stress, performance, and selfhood.

03

Human–technology interaction

We study how interfaces and feedback shape what measurements appear to mean—and how those meanings influence behaviour and self-understanding.

04

Future interfaces

We prototype embodied and interactive systems that expose uncertainty, invite reflection, and support responsible engagement.

02 / Approach

Interdisciplinary by design.

Our work combines methods from human–computer interaction, cognitive psychology, design research, data sensemaking, biomedical signal processing, and engineering. Depending on the question, a project may involve controlled studies, qualitative inquiry, participatory workshops, interface design, or prototyping.

Cognitive Futures Symposium

Save the date

A meeting place for cognitive futures.

A half-day symposium bringing together researchers, companies, and practitioners working across neurotechnology, sensing, cognitive science, data sensemaking, HCI, design, engineering, and related fields.

Date7 December 2026
FormatHalf day
VenueAula, TU Delft
RegistrationDetails to follow

01 / Programme

Different formats, one shared conversation.

01

Keynote

An invited perspective on a critical question for the future of cognitive technologies.

02

Panel

A cross-sector conversation connecting research, technology development, interpretation, and responsibility.

03

Posters

A forum for researchers and students to share current work, early findings, methods, and open questions.

04

Workshops

Small-group sessions for exploring shared challenges, methods, prototypes, and future collaborations.

02 / Who should join

Open to intersecting domains.

We welcome researchers at every career stage whose work touches cognitive psychology, neuroscience, HCI, design, data visualisation and sensemaking, ethics, wearable computing, biosensing, mechanical engineering, or adjacent areas.

We also invite companies developing consumer neurotechnology, physiological sensing, wearables, research hardware, cognitive-data tools, and interfaces for complex measurements.

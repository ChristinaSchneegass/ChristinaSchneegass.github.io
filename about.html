About

A lab for uncertain cognitive futures.

Cognitive Futures Lab examines emerging relationships between people, cognitive measurements, and interactive technologies—without assuming that more measurement automatically produces better understanding.

01 / Mission

Make interpretation part of the research.

As cognitive and physiological sensing enters consumer products, measurements can quickly become claims about who people are, how they feel, or how well they perform. We investigate the assumptions, interfaces, and social contexts that turn measurements into meaning.

Our aim is to create knowledge and technologies that make uncertainty visible, support human agency, and enable more responsible cognitive futures.

02 / Institutions

TU Delft

Lab coordination and interdisciplinary research across design, HCI, cognition, and human–machine systems.

TU/e

Expertise in data sensemaking, pervasive health, physiological computing, and biomedical signal processing.

University of Twente

Expertise in tangible, embedded, and embodied interaction and the physicalisation of biosignal data.

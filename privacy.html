Privacy

Privacy on this website.

This site is designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information we collect

The website does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, account registration, contact forms, or a newsletter signup. When you follow an email link, your email application handles the message and you choose what information to send.

Hosting

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to provide and secure its hosting service. Please refer to GitHub’s own privacy statement for details.

External links

The site links to university profiles, partner websites, and 4TU.NIRICT. Those websites have their own privacy practices.
